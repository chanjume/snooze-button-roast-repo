# Snooze Button Roast

[![make.fun bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-bounty-blue)](https://make.fun)
[![entry](https://img.shields.io/badge/entry-one--line-green)](https://make.fun)
[![license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-lightgrey)](LICENSE)

One line, 132 characters: an alarm clock roasts the snooze button —
it rang at 6 AM on Saturday like a smoke detector with a grudge, and
there was no fire, no smoke, only the weekend going up in flames.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on Saturday like a smoke detector with a grudge — no fire, no smoke, just my weekend going up in flames.
